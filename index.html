Mathematical & biophysical foundations

Methods

Pipelines are built on explicit derivations implemented in Python rather than GUI-only tools.

Local Distance Difference Test (pLDDT)

Superposition-free local agreement over Cα pairs within 15 Å:

$$\text{LDDT}=\frac{1}{4|D|}\sum_{(i,j)\in D}\sum_{t\in\{0.5,1,2,4\}}\mathbb{I}\big(|d^{\text{pred}}_{ij}-d^{\text{true}}_{ij}|\le t\big)$$

Backbone dihedrals (φ, ψ)

From four atoms with bond vectors \(\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2,\mathbf v_3\) and normals \(\mathbf n_1=\mathbf v_1\times\mathbf v_2\), \(\mathbf n_2=\mathbf v_2\times\mathbf v_3\):

$$\theta=\operatorname{atan2}\!\Big(\big(\mathbf n_1\times\mathbf n_2\big)\cdot\tfrac{\mathbf v_2}{\lVert\mathbf v_2\rVert},\;\mathbf n_1\cdot\mathbf n_2\Big)$$

Cα contact topology

Euclidean distance matrix with a non-trivial contact definition:

$$D_{ij}=\lVert\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j\rVert_2,\qquad D_{ij}\le 8\,\text{Å}\;\wedge\;|i-j|\ge 6$$

Predicted Aligned Error

Expected position error of residue \(j\) when aligned on the frame of residue \(i\):

$$\text{PAE}_{ij}=\mathbb E\big[\lVert\mathbf x^{\text{true}}_j-\mathbf T_i\,\mathbf x^{\text{pred}}_j\rVert\big]$$

Kabsch superposition & RMSD

Optimal rotation from the SVD of the covariance \(H=P^{\top}Q=U\Sigma V^{\top}\):

$$R=V\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,\det(VU^{\top}))\,U^{\top},\quad \text{RMSD}=\sqrt{\tfrac1N\textstyle\sum_i\lVert R\mathbf p_i-\mathbf q_i\rVert^2}$$

Shannon conservation

Per-column entropy of the multiple sequence alignment (H = 0 ⇒ invariant):

$$H_c=-\sum_{a}p_{a,c}\log_2 p_{a,c}$$
