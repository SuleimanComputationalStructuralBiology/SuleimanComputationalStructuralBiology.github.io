Project Overview & Systems Biology Context

AKT1 (RAC-alpha serine/threonine-protein kinase) is the central hub kinase identified by co-expression network analysis of triple-negative breast cancer. Its 3D structure was predicted with AlphaFold2 (ColabFold, MMseqs2 MSA) and characterised with custom Python routines for per-residue confidence, backbone dihedral geometry and Cα contact topology.

Key Structural Findings

  • Kinase core is predicted with very high confidence while the PH-domain linker and C-terminal regulatory tail are intrinsically disordered (pLDDT < 50).
  • Ramachandran analysis: 24.6% α-helix, 32.9% β-strand, 3.1% left-handed/Gly, 39.4% loops.
  • 573 long-range Cα contacts (1.4% of all pairs); distance range 3.0–76.7 Å.
