STN7 / STN8 Chloroplast Kinases

Comparative structural, evolutionary & complex modelling of thylakoid kinases — Arabidopsis thaliana (STN7: Q9S713, STN8: Q9LZV4)

Project Overview & Photosynthetic State Transitions

STN7 drives PSII↔PSI state transitions through LHCII phosphorylation, while STN8 phosphorylates PSII core proteins during high-light repair. This pipeline retrieves AlphaFold models and orthologs across dicots, monocots and green algae, performs MSA, phylogeny, SVD superposition, domain-level confidence statistics, hydropathy profiling, motif annotation and models the STN7–STN8 heterodimer with AlphaFold2-multimer; ligand libraries are prepared for docking.

Key Structural & Evolutionary Discoveries

  • Arabidopsis STN7 and STN8 share 36.1% sequence identity, consistent with an ancient duplication.
  • 57.02% of alignment columns are perfectly conserved (H = 0) across land plants and algae.
  • Kinase domains are far more confident than transit peptides / tails (t-test p < 1e-40 for both).
  • AF2-multimer heterodimer: ipTM 0.75, pTM 0.82, 2179 inter-chain contact pairs (≤ 6.0 Å).
  • No classic TM helix (> 1.6): max Kyte–Doolittle peaks 1.49 (STN7) and 1.59 (STN8); profiles correlate (Pearson r = 0.2039, p = 0.0419).
